- Seeded `importlinter` gate for fastapi: `scripts/check-import-layers.sh` runs `lint-imports` against the layer contract in `[tool.importlinter]` and fails on a broken contract, a missing contract, a missing tool or a run that kept no contract. It is registered in `gates.txt`, `ci-gates.sh`, lefthook pre-push, both CI hosts and `verify-harness.sh`. The gate ratchet refuses to drop or weaken `tool.importlinter` and refuses a new `.importlinter` or `setup.cfg` that could shadow it.

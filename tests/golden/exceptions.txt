# Paths allowed to differ from the golden hashes.
# Format: <combo|*> <path> # <reason>
# <combo> is <stack>-<ci>, for example nextjs-github.
#
# What the golden covers
#   One entry per file the scaffold markdown writes verbatim: the first fence after a
#   path heading, a "Create `<path>`:" line, or a heading marked "Empty file".
#   .claude/settings.json and lefthook.yml also carry an inline-bold heading in
#   harness-kit-enforcement.md (Step A rules, Step B2 wiring). Those headings hold rules, not
#   a body: the golden covers only the Step A / Step B2 fence in the stack's variant kit
#   (harness-kit-ts.md or harness-kit-fastapi.md). tests/golden/unfenced.txt lists the headings
#   without a body, and extract-fences.test.sh pins that the variant fence is the golden entry.
#
# What `check-golden.sh --tree` leaves out of a generated project (created at runtime)
#   lockfiles (pnpm-lock.yaml, package-lock.json, yarn.lock), node_modules/,
#   src/components/ui/ (shadcn output), .claude/harness.json (seeded_at, origin_hash),
#   .claude/.harness-base/ (snapshot of the seeded files).
# Also relaxed in --tree mode: a placeholder matches one line of text, AGENTS.md may carry
#   appended sections, package.json may carry added dependencies, and the files in
#   tests/golden/formatter-owned.txt are compared after their formatter runs.
